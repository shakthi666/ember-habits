Privacy Policy — Ember

Effective date: June 9, 2026

Ember ("the app") is a free habit-tracking app for Android, supported by advertising. This policy explains what information is and is not handled when you use the app.

Information we collect

The app itself does not collect, store on our servers, or transmit any personal information. Your habits, check-ins, and streaks are stored only on your device. We have no servers, no accounts, and no access to anything you enter. Deleting the app deletes all of your data.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service operated by Google. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect certain data from your device, including your device's Advertising ID, IP address, and general device information. This collection is performed by Google under Google's own policies, not by us.

You can learn how Google uses this data here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings under Settings > Google > Ads.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 18 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and deletion

We hold no user data, so there is nothing for us to share, sell, or delete. Your habit data lives only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. Data handled by Google AdMob is governed by Google's policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.
